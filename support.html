02

What to include

Please do not send passwords. For account or purchase issues, include:

  • the email address used for your Gymestix account;
  • your device type and operating-system version;
  • a short description of what happened and when;
  • screenshots, if they help explain the issue;
  • for purchase issues, the store order reference if available.
03

Account deletion and privacy

You can delete your account in the app when signed in. If you cannot access the app, email us with the subject Gymestix Account Deletion Request and include the email address linked to the account.

For privacy details, including data use, retention, and your rights, read the Privacy Policy.

04

Purchases, ads, and billing

If an ad-free purchase or store transaction is not recognized, contact us with your store order reference. Refunds and billing adjustments may also require Apple App Store or Google Play support, depending on where the purchase was made.

If you are using the free version, device-level advertising controls and in-app privacy choices may affect the ads shown to you.
