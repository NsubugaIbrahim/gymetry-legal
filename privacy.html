The short version

Gymeflix stores the fitness information you enter, can sync it to a Supabase-backed account, uses Google Mobile Ads to display ads, and uses Google Play Billing for the optional ad-free purchase on Android. We do not sell your personal or sensitive fitness data for money, and we do not intentionally send your profile, BMI, or workout records to advertisers.

01

Scope and who we are

This Privacy Policy applies to the Gymeflix fitness and workout application, which may also be identified as Gymetry in package names, source code, links, or technical records (together, the “App”). “Gymeflix,” “we,” “us,” and “our” refer to the developer and publisher identified on the App’s official store listing.

This policy covers the Android and iOS versions of the App, the account and sync services used by the App, and this privacy-policy website. It does not govern a third-party service’s independent processing under its own privacy policy.

Core service Fitness data you control

Your profile, measurements, routines, completed sets, sessions, and progress records.

Cloud sync Optional account storage

Supabase provides authentication, database sync, password recovery, and profile-image storage.

Monetization Ads or ad-free

Google serves ads; eligible Android users can make a one-time Google Play purchase to remove them.

03

How information is collected

Information you provide

We receive information when you register, complete your profile, enter measurements, build a training program, log a workout, select a profile image, change settings, make a purchase, or contact us.

Information from a sign-in provider

If you choose Google, Facebook, Apple, or X sign-in, the provider and Supabase may give the App an account identifier and information you authorized, such as your email address, display name, or profile image. The exact information depends on the provider and your settings with that provider. We do not receive your third-party account password.

Information collected automatically

The Google Mobile Ads SDK automatically processes certain device, network, interaction, diagnostic, and advertising-identifier data when ads are requested or displayed. Our Supabase service also receives ordinary network and security information, including IP addresses and request logs, when the App connects to it.

On-device generation

The App calculates BMI-related values, progress metrics, routine compliance, training efficiency, and other workout statistics from the information you enter. These derived values may be stored locally and synced with your account.

05

Advertising and identifiers

The free version of Gymeflix uses Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads for banner and interstitial advertising. Google states that its Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and share IP address, user product interactions, diagnostic information, Android advertising ID, app-set ID, and other device or account identifiers for advertising, analytics, and fraud-prevention purposes.

Ads may be personalized where permitted by law and your consent or device settings. Even when personalization is unavailable or disabled, contextual or limited ads may still use information such as the App, approximate location inferred from IP, and the content area in which an ad appears.

Separation of fitness and ad data: we do not intentionally pass your name, email address, birthday, profile photo, body measurements, BMI records, workout history, or fitness goals to Google as ad-targeting parameters.

You can reset or delete the Android advertising ID in your device’s privacy settings, limit tracking through iOS settings where available, use Google’s in-ad privacy controls, or purchase the ad-free option where offered. Removing ads stops future ad requests from the App after the entitlement is recognized, but does not automatically delete data previously processed by Google.

Depending on local law, Google’s processing for interest-based advertising may be treated as “sharing” or “targeted advertising.” We do not sell personal or sensitive fitness data for monetary consideration.

08

Data retention

We use the following retention principles:

  • On-device records remain until you delete them, use the account-deletion control, clear App data, or uninstall the App. Copies may remain in device backups controlled by your operating-system or backup provider.
  • Account and synced fitness records are generally kept while your account is active and until deletion is requested or the data is no longer needed to provide the App.
  • Profile images stored for account sync are kept while needed for your profile and are removed when the corresponding deletion request is completed, subject to backup limitations.
  • Authentication and security records may be retained for a limited period where reasonably necessary to secure the service, investigate abuse, resolve disputes, or comply with law.
  • Purchase records are retained by Google under its policies. We may retain entitlement and transaction-reference information needed to honor purchases, prevent fraud, and meet legal obligations.
  • Advertising data is retained by Google and its authorized partners under their own policies and the consent choices that apply.
  • Deletion and support communications may be retained long enough to complete your request, document compliance, prevent fraud, and resolve disputes.

Deleted information may remain temporarily in restricted backups or logs until routine deletion cycles complete. We will not use retained backup data for ordinary product or advertising purposes and will delete or isolate it as required by applicable law.

09

Delete your account or data

In the App

  1. Open Gymeflix and sign in to the account you want to delete.
  2. Open your profile or account screen and select Delete Account.
  3. Review the warning and confirm deletion. The App will remove local records and initiate removal of associated synced App data.

From the web or if you cannot access the App

Send an email to the privacy address in Contact us with the subject Gymeflix Account Deletion Request. Include the email address associated with the account and enough information for us to verify ownership. Never send your password or full payment-card details.

Go to deletion contact

What happens next

After verification, we will delete or de-identify the Gymeflix authentication account, profile, stored profile image, synced workouts, routines, body measurements, BMI records, and other account-linked App data, unless specific information must be retained for security, fraud prevention, transaction records, dispute resolution, or another legal obligation. We will explain any applicable exception. Deleting Gymeflix does not delete your separate Google, Facebook, Apple, X, or app-store account.

Uninstalling the App alone removes ordinary local App data but does not automatically delete an online account or data already synced to the service. Use one of the deletion methods above for online deletion.

Your privacy choices

  • Profile and fitness data: review or update available information in the profile and fitness screens.
  • Cloud sync: do not sign in or provide account information if you do not want to use online account features. Network-dependent features may then be unavailable.
  • Profile image: do not select an image, replace it in your profile, or request deletion.
  • Notifications: deny permission, disable reminders in the App where available, or turn them off in system settings.
  • Advertising: use device advertising controls, Google’s ad controls, and any consent or privacy-options message made available to you.
  • Ad-free purchase: purchase the optional product where available to stop ads in the App after the entitlement is confirmed.
  • Social sign-in: revoke Gymeflix access through the selected provider’s account settings. Revocation does not by itself delete your Gymeflix account.
  • Account deletion: use the methods described in the deletion section.

12

How we protect information

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards appropriate to the nature of the information. These include encrypted HTTPS connections for backend requests, private App storage for local authentication tokens and databases, authenticated requests for account-linked sync, access controls on backend data, and data-minimization practices. Profile-image uploads are sent using encrypted connections.

No device, transmission, or storage system is completely secure. You are responsible for protecting your device, email account, sign-in-provider account, and password. Contact us promptly if you believe your Gymeflix account has been compromised.

Gymeflix and its service providers may process information in countries other than the country where you live. Those countries may have different data-protection laws. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards for international transfers, such as adequacy decisions, contractual protections, or other lawful transfer mechanisms. 14

Children’s privacy

Gymeflix is a general-audience fitness App and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from a child under 13. Where local law sets a higher age for valid consent, a user below that age should use account features only with authorization from a parent or guardian.

If you believe a child provided personal information without the required permission, contact us. We will investigate and delete the information when legally required. If the App is later offered specifically to children, this policy and the App’s data practices will be updated before that offering.

15

Fitness information is not medical advice

Height, weight, BMI, estimated body-fat percentage, workout performance, and related information can be sensitive. Gymeflix uses these details to provide user-requested fitness features and does not intentionally disclose them to advertisers for ad targeting. The App does not currently connect to Health Connect, Apple Health, healthcare providers, insurers, or employers.

App calculations are estimates for general fitness and informational purposes. They are not medical records, diagnoses, or treatment advice. Consult a qualified professional for medical concerns and before beginning a program when appropriate.
